# Elsepin identity — implementation brief

Date: 2026-10-26. Language/market assumption: English, United States; worldwide distribution has not been cleared. This is a working product choice, **HOLD for commercial clearance**, not a legal opinion or a claim of exclusivity.

## Naming brief

An iPhone location-control app with routes, favorites, personalization and optional provider observations. Desired impression: precise, playful, independent, considered. Avoid names that promise invisibility, undetectability or surveillance. Short enough for a Home Screen label; distinct from the crowded word Shift and the separate paid Veypax product.

## Category language and benefit ladder

Current product references are [Locus](https://github.com/ChrisMack32/Locus) and the underlying [idevice](https://github.com/jkcoxson/idevice) project. “GPS”, “location”, “spoof”, “geo” and generic movement words describe mechanisms more readily than a memorable identity. The benefit ladder is: select a simulated location → choose a route and pace → reduce setup friction → feel in control → make the experience your own. This is positioning judgment, not a claim about users' measured preferences.

The shortlist is a design decision, not a weighted commercial-clearance score. No candidate has passed every hard gate, so numerical scores would imply unsupported precision.

## Recommendation and real contexts

Use **Elsepin** as the working identity selected for implementation. Homepage: “Elsepin — Make your next move.” App: “Open Elsepin.” Notification: “Elsepin pairing code.” Support: “Elsepin → Settings → Connection.” Widget: “Start manually in Elsepin.” No registrability, exclusive rights or search ranking is promised.

## Visual identity

Prism Jumpgate is the primary mark. Retain its approved purple/mint portal pixels; do not redraw it. The website uses warm cream, near-black type and generous space. Ion uses #10170D canvas, #20291C solid surfaces and #D7FF4F action color. Other themes retain their own palettes rather than becoming Ion recolors.

Typography is system-first in the app, with accessible relative text size and optional rounded, serif and monospaced styles. Website headlines combine a compact sans serif with a restrained italic serif. No web font dependency or looping motion is needed. Controls remain recognizable, labels brief, and advanced details live in help pages.
